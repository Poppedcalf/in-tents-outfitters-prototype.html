Questions

Before you book

What do I actually get?

A fully equipped camp: spacious tents with comfortable beds and linen, a private en-suite at the camp, and everything set up and ready when you arrive.

Who sets it up and packs it away?

We do. The camp is delivered, set up, and collected afterwards. You just enjoy the bush.

Where can you set up?

Anywhere you choose. We're based in Kariba and work around the lake and the surrounding bush. Tell us your location when you enquire.

What can I use a camp for?

Hunting, fishing trips, safaris, bush retreats, and extra accommodation for family and friends.

How do I get a price and check dates?

Send us your dates, group size and location on WhatsApp or through the form below. We'll come back with availability and a quote.
