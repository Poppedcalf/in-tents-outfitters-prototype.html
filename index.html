    Our story

    26 years on Lake Kariba & beyond

    26+Years in Kariba
    100%Mobile
    AnyWild location

    For 26 years our Kariba base was Warthogs Safari Camp, a lakeshore camp where travellers, overlanders and safari groups stayed on the edge of a busy wildlife corridor, with elephant, zebra and hippo regularly passing through.

    Today that experience travels. As In Tents Outfitters we bring the same comfort and know-how to wherever you want to be, from Lake Kariba to Matusadona, Mana Pools and private concessions, and handle the delivery, set-up and pack-down so you can simply enjoy the bush.

    Questions

    Before you book

    What do I actually get?

    A fully equipped camp: spacious tents with comfortable beds and linen, a private en-suite at the camp, and everything set up and ready when you arrive. Kitchen, power and dining can be added.

    Who sets it up and packs it away?

    We do. The camp is delivered, set up and collected afterwards.

    Where can you set up?

    Anywhere you choose. We're based in Kariba and work around the lake, Matusadona, Mana Pools and elsewhere in Zimbabwe. Tell us your location when you enquire.

    What can I use a camp for?

    Hunting, fishing trips, safaris, bush retreats, weddings and events, and extra accommodation for family and friends.

    How do I get a price?

    Use the camp builder or message us on WhatsApp with your dates, group size and location. We'll come back with availability and a quote.
